❓ FAQ

Is it free? Any ads or microtransactions?

100% free. No ads, no real-money purchases. Everything is earned by playing.

Does my progress save?

Yes, automatically on your device. Use ⚙️ Settings → Export to move your save to another device.

How do I turn the music off?

Use the "Play without music" button above, or ⚙️ Settings / ⏸ Pause menu in-game. Sound effects can be toggled separately.

Is this BTD?

No. Slimefall is an original game inspired by the depth of great tower defense games. All towers, enemies, maps, art and music are original.

Can I contribute?

Yes! It's open source (MIT). See GitHub.
